# Credits

The music on koan's demo server, demo.koan.rocks. Every release is published
by its artist under a licence that allows it to be shared, including
commercially. Track tags have been rewritten so that every artist is credited
on their own, with each release's licence in its files; the FLAC releases are
otherwise unchanged, and the AAC, MP3 and Opus releases are encoded from the
artists' FLAC.

- Kimiko Ishizaka – *J.S. Bach: “Open” Goldberg Variations, BWV 988* (2012),
  three tracks. CC0 1.0. https://archive.org/details/OpenGoldbergVariations
- Stellardrone – *Light Years* (Energostatic Records, 2013). CC BY 3.0.
  https://archive.org/details/Stellardrone-LightYears
- Monsieur LowTec – *Great LowTec Xperience* (Oisoft Rekords, 2005). CC BY 2.5.
  https://archive.org/details/MonsieurLowtec. The release has no cover; the
  one here is typographic, made for this library.
- Josh Woodward – *Addressed to the Stars* (2016). CC BY 4.0.
  https://archive.org/details/pandacd-706-addressed-to-the-stars
- Broke For Free – *Directionless EP* (2011). CC BY 3.0.
  https://archive.org/details/DirectionlessEP
- Chud011 & rolemusic – *Collider* (2014). CC BY 4.0.
  https://archive.org/details/Collider
- Various Artists – *kōan Demo Sampler* (2026): a compilation made for this
  library, of one track from each release above, under each track's own
  licence. Its cover, like Monsieur LowTec's, was made for it.
